Privacy Policy

RC Timeline (Real Care Timeline) — Last updated: 18 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Colin Laffan (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the RC Timeline app (“the App”). We take your privacy seriously, particularly given the sensitive nature of the care records this App is designed to hold.

If you have any questions, contact us at laffaner@gmail.com.

1. Who We Are

RC Timeline is an iOS and iPadOS app developed and operated by Colin Laffan, an independent software developer. The App is designed to help parents and carers record and organise care events for their dependants.

For the purposes of UK and EU data protection law, Colin Laffan is the data controller.

2. Data We Collect

Account data

  • Email address — used to create and identify your account. If you sign in with Apple, we receive a verified email address (or an Apple-relayed address if you choose to hide your email).

Care records you create

  • Subjects — the name, date of birth, optional profile photo, and notes you enter for each person you are tracking care for.
  • Timeline events — event title, date, event type, organisation, professional name, and notes you enter for each care event.
  • Documents — files (photos, PDFs) you attach to events. These are stored in your private cloud storage.

Automatically collected data

  • Crash reports — if the App crashes, an anonymised crash report is sent automatically. This includes device type, iOS version, and a stack trace but does not include your care records or personal details.
  • Usage events — anonymised usage signals such as app opened, event created, document uploaded, and scan initiated. No care record content is included in these signals.

Data collected when using AI extraction

When you use the document scan feature, the text extracted from your document (via on-device OCR) is sent to an AI service to pre-fill an event form. If you have entered a subject name in the App, that name may also be sent to improve extraction accuracy. See Third-Party Services for details.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your care records are stored in a private Supabase database protected by Row Level Security — only your account can read or write your data. Documents are stored in private Supabase Storage with equivalent access controls.

Your session token is stored in the iOS Keychain with the kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly attribute, meaning it cannot be backed up to iCloud or accessed when the device is locked.

The App implements jailbreak detection and will not operate on compromised devices.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the App, which permanently and irreversibly deletes all your care records, documents, and account data from our servers.

6. Children’s Data

RC Timeline is used by parents and carers to record information about their dependants — it is not directed at children and children do not use it directly. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently received such data, please contact us at laffaner@gmail.com.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (most data can be edited directly in the App).
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data (available directly in the App via Profile → Delete Account).
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction — ask us to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent for AI extraction at any time by not using the scan feature.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at laffaner@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
